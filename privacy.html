Varzishh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026 · Operated by Midnight Developers

Varzishh is a home-workout app. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how you can get it back or delete it. It covers the Android app and the Varzishh backend service.

Who we are

Varzishh is operated by Midnight Developers. For any privacy question, or to exercise any right described here, contact us at midnightdeployers@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhy we hold it
Account identity — your name, email address and Google account identifier, received from Google Sign-In To create your account and let you sign in again on another device.
Fitness profile — age range, and optionally gender, height, weight, experience level and preferred units To generate a workout plan suited to you. Everything except age range is optional and the app works without it.
Training preferences — training days, session length, available space, and the equipment you own To choose exercises you can actually perform where you train.
Workout activity — sessions you start and complete, and the sets and repetitions you record To show your progress and adjust future sessions.
Notification token — a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token, only if you allow notifications To send workout reminders you asked for. Revoking the Android notification permission stops this.

What we do not collect

Varzishh contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party marketing tools. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, or device identifiers for advertising. We do not build advertising profiles, and we never sell or rent your data. The app requests only two Android permissions: internet access, and — with your consent — notifications.

Who your data is shared with

We share data only with the infrastructure providers required to run the service, never for their own purposes:

ProviderRole
Google (Firebase Authentication)Verifies your Google sign-in.
Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging)Delivers notifications you opted into.
RenderHosts the Varzishh backend service.
NeonHosts the database, located in Singapore.

We may disclose data if legally required to do so.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Singapore (Asia Pacific). Google's authentication and messaging services may process data in other countries under their own terms.

Your rights

You do not need to email us to exercise these — both are built into the app, under Profile:

  • Export your data. Request a machine-readable copy of everything associated with your account.
  • Delete your account. This removes your profile, preferences, plans and workout history. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

You may also contact us to ask what we hold about you or to have it corrected.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the associated records are removed from our live database. Encrypted backups may retain copies briefly before rotating out.

Security

All traffic between the app and our service is encrypted with HTTPS, and database connections are encrypted with TLS. Sign-in is handled by Google — we never see or store your Google password.

Age

Varzishh is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Any significant change will be communicated in the app before it takes effect.
